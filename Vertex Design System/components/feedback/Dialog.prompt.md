Modal for confirmations and enquiry results.

```jsx
<Dialog open={open} eyebrow="Build slot" title="Your request is registered."
  onClose={close} footer={<Button onClick={close}>Close</Button>}>
  A member of the studio will be in contact within two working days.
</Dialog>
```

Scrim is Vertex Black at 82% with a 3px blur. Dialogs are the only surface allowed a shadow.
