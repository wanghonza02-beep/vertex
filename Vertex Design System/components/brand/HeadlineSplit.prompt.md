The brand's headline signature. Never mix the two faces inside one clause.

```jsx
<HeadlineSplit size="hero" luxury="Immortal Icons." tech="Modern Muscle." />
<HeadlineSplit size="section" luxury="Stripped to bare metal." tech="Rebuilt to spec." />
```
