The one element Vertex is remembered by. Use for horsepower, 0–100 times, donor years, man-hours, build counts.

```jsx
<SpecNumber value={640} unit="hp" label="Power output" tone="gold" countUp />
<SpecNumber value="3.2" unit="sec" label="0–100 km/h" />
<SpecNumber value={1989} label="Donor year" size="lg" />
```

Group 3–4 in a row with 70px gaps. At most one `tone="gold"` per group — the headline stat. Never round the corners of the leader line or thicken it past 1px.
